Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 Oct 2026

Who we are

Ozplore ("we", "us", "our") is a digital stamp-passport app for Australian road trips. It is operated by Swapnil Naik, a sole trader (ABN 59 976 517 612). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights. Questions: ozplore@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in, we collect your email address (used to send you a one-time sign-in code) and a display name you choose. Your display name is shown publicly — for example on the leaderboard and, if you choose, as credit next to places you suggest.

Location information. With your permission, we access your device's location to:

  • show how far a stop is from you while you are viewing it, and power the "near me" feature; and
  • confirm you're at a stop when you check in ("Stamp — I'm here now").

We only access location while the app is open (foreground). We do not track your location in the background. Distances are worked out on your device. Your device's coordinates are sent to our servers only when you check in on the spot, so we can confirm you are within range of the stop. We use them for that check and do not store them. Claiming a place you've visited before does not send your location.

Activity information. We record which stops you collect, whether you checked in on the spot (GPS) or claimed a place you'd previously visited, the date and time of collection, and the points earned. If you claim a place you visited before, you can also add the month and year of that visit. This is your passport and progress.

Check-in photos. If you choose to add a photo to a stamp, using your camera or photo library (only when you tap to add one), we store it in a private area of our storage provider, linked to your account and that stop. It is retrieved only through your signed-in account in the app. Photos are not currently end-to-end encrypted, so a person with administrative access to our storage could technically open them. We do not view them. You can replace or remove a photo at any time. If you share a stamp card, the card is created on your device and shared through the app you choose.

Content you provide. If you suggest a place, we collect what you enter (name, town, category, a map link, coordinates, and any note) and your preference about whether to be credited publicly. We also record places you star or save.

Device preferences. Some settings (like light/dark theme) are stored only on your device, not on our servers.

Usage information. To understand how the app is used and to improve it, we collect basic usage events — for example, which screens and stops are viewed, whether a search returns results, when you tap "Directions", and when a stamp is collected. These events are tied to a random identifier generated on your device, not to your account or email. We do not link this data to your account and we do not use it to identify you. We do not record the words you type into search. (App versions before 1.1.1 recorded the search words when a search found nothing; those records are deleted after 12 months like all usage events.) We keep usage events for 12 months.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect payment information (Ozplore is free).
  • We do not track your location in the background, and we do not keep a history of where you have been.
  • We do not access your contacts or microphone. We access your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a check-in photo.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or ad-tracking.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Why we collect it (purpose)

To provide the core service: letting you collect stamps, verify visits, track progress and rank, appear on leaderboards, contribute suggestions, and receive credit if you choose, and to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. We rely on your consent (which you can withdraw) and our legitimate interest in operating the app.

Who we share it with (service providers)

We use trusted third parties to run Ozplore. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — stores your account, activity, photo and usage data, and provides authentication. Hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • Resend — sends your one-time sign-in code emails.
  • Apple Maps / Google Maps — when you tap "Directions", we open Apple Maps (on iPhone) or Google Maps (on Android) with the stop's coordinates. Your use of those apps is subject to Apple's or Google's privacy policy.
  • Expo — the framework we use to build and deliver the app.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribute the app and may collect their own information under their own privacy policies.

We do not otherwise share your personal information, except where required by law.

Public information

Your display name and points are visible to other users on the leaderboard. If you suggest a place and choose to be credited, your display name may appear publicly next to that place. You can choose not to be credited when you submit a suggestion. Please don't use a display name that reveals information you'd rather keep private.

Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we'll remove your personal data, including your check-ins and photos, within 30 days of your request (copies held by our service providers or in backups can take up to 90 days to be fully purged). To request deletion, see our account deletion page. We may also keep what we must for legal reasons; except anything we must keep for legal reasons. Approved community suggestions may remain in the app as places, but can be disassociated from your identity on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these, contact ozplore@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, and may need to confirm your identity first. Australian users are protected under the Privacy Act 1988; you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Location permission

You control location access through your device settings, and can turn it off or withdraw consent at any time. Without location, you can still browse stops and claim places you've visited, but on-the-spot check-in and "near me" won't work.

Children

Ozplore is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted connections and access controls via Supabase) to protect your information. No method is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Users outside Australia

Ozplore is operated from Australia and is designed for people exploring Australian locations, including international visitors. If you use Ozplore from outside Australia, your information is handled in Australia and in the other countries listed below. This section applies in addition to the rest of this Privacy Policy. If it conflicts with another part of this Policy, this section prevails for users outside Australia.
