Delete Your Account & Data

You can ask us to delete your Ozplore account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

Email us at ozplore@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Ozplore account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We'll confirm and process your request.

Because Ozplore signs you in with your email address, requesting from that same email helps us verify the account is yours.

What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we remove the personal data we hold about you, including:

  • Your account and email address
  • Your display name
  • Your collected stamps, points, and activity history
  • Your saved and starred items

What may be kept

  • Approved place suggestions: if you suggested a place that was added to Ozplore, the place itself may remain in the app as part of the shared catalogue, but it will be disassociated from your identity (any public credit removed on request).
  • Legal or security records: we may retain limited information where required by law, or to prevent fraud and abuse, for as long as necessary.

Timing

We aim to action deletion requests within 30 days. Some data held by our service providers or in backups may take up to 90 days to be fully purged.
